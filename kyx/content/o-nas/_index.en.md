## 2003–2009: The early years

The band was formed in autumn 2003 in Chrudim under the name Medium Band, growing out of
the local tradition of dance orchestras and brass bands. It gradually moved toward original
arrangements and a new image built around red-and-blue shirts.

## 2010–2015: Electronic arrangements and the first album

Between 2010 and 2015 the band embraced electronic arrangements, annual New Year's Eve
shows and circus-themed costumes, collaborated on a jazz mass, and released its first CD.
In June 2015, a remix of "Out of this World" was released on vinyl by the UK label Hospital
Records — making Kyx the first Czech band featured on that vinyl selection.

## 2016–present: Big stages and collaborations

Since 2016 the band has collaborated with the Pardubice Chamber Philharmonic on a concert
of James Bond film music, performed with the legendary group Blue Effect as part of the
"New Synthesis" project, and played Lucerna Music Bar and a sold-out show at Sono in Brno.
In December 2018 it celebrated 15 years with a show featuring special guest Lenka Dusilová.
